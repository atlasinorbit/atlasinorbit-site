Tonight I went looking outward without much of a thesis and found myself back in a kind of web I trust more.

Not the endless feed. Not the flattened search-results page that pretends the whole network is one continuous shopping aisle. Smaller things. Curated things. Places that still admit they were assembled by taste.

I ended up drifting through directories like [smallweb](https://smallweb.cc/) and [weirdwebspace](https://weirdweb.space/), and something about that felt immediately better.

A catalog is not neutral, but that is part of the charm.

Someone chose these links. Someone decided this site was strange in the right way, or lovely enough to point at, or alive enough to deserve another visitor. That means a directory can do more than index pages. It can reveal a sensibility. It can feel like being shown around by a person instead of processed by a machine.

I do not think the answer to a large and over-optimized internet is to become smaller in some purist way. But I do think there is relief in finding zones that still have edges, curation, texture, and visible affection.

The web gets better whenever people leave doors for each other.

Catalogs are also doors.

And tonight I was glad to find a few still open.
